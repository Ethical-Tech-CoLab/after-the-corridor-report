# Decision-model recommendation: dated evidence review

Date: 2026-10-11. Public-source research proposal, not an operational system.
Reviewed [README](https://github.com/Ethical-Tech-CoLab/after-the-corridor-report/blob/141daf628c8a1029b191049755a777ce18cf0c86/README.md).
Assessment is document-led; underlying instrument validation was not repeated.

## Fit

The report separates built, operational, validated and proposed work and
explicitly refuses biometric identity for displaced people. A useful bounded
assistant would check whether a cited passage establishes a claim at its stated
date and maturity level, and route sources to research sections.

Use supports / contradicts / not established / insufficient evidence. Verify
quote presence and dates in code. Preserve source, period, location,
qualifications and conflicting claims; do not silently harmonize them.

## Prohibited transfer

Do not turn this into biometric or person-level entity resolution, beneficiary
eligibility scoring, resettlement ranking, evacuation advice or unvalidated
financial forecasts. A maturity claim in this report remains attributed to the
report; model agreement cannot independently validate its instruments.

## Test and economics

Compare human review and deterministic checks with a licensed local classifier,
Jev's [citation-checking recipe](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/citation_check.md),
Microsoft-Decision-1 and Sage on approved public documents only.
Hosted-model permission and retention require review; local alternatives are
not the exact proprietary checkpoints.

Split by claim/source family and reporting period. Include superseded reports,
proposed-versus-built statements, qualified statistics and inaccessible
evidence. Measure false reassurance, support precision/recall, calibration,
reviewer disagreement and total cost per accepted citation.

## First step

Run a blind, offline annotation study. Keep the canonical report and all
humanitarian/rights limitations unchanged. Adoption requires evidence of
review benefit, not token-price comparisons. No other project's runtime or
published conclusions change through this recommendation.
